# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-27

### Features
- First release: `guest-card` and `guest-mini-card`, what a guest or a babysitter needs on one card. The guest Wi-Fi as a QR code (it opens full screen, centred), its password and the door code behind a button (masked again after 30 seconds and when the card leaves the page), the front door lock and the alarm as tiles, the evening's plan from a calendar and a list, and contacts to call (with the emergency number). A guest mode switch (`input_boolean`) with an optional end time drives the status and the *End guest mode* button. Illustrated header: a house whose door opens with guest mode, a porch light that breathes, stars by night. The mini card shows the state and the network on two lines, its QR code one tap away. Visual editor, light and dark themes, 22 languages.
